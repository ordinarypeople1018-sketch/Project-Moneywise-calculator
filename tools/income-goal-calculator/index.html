How this calculator works

Instead of asking "how much can I save?", this calculator asks "how much do I need to earn?". It is useful when setting a savings goal and working backwards to the salary or revenue required.

The formula

Net needed = Expenses + Savings target
Gross needed = Net needed ÷ (1 − Tax rate)

Worked example

To save $800 a month while spending $2,500, with a 20% tax rate, you need $4,125 gross per month.
