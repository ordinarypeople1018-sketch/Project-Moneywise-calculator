How this calculator works

A monthly budget is the single most useful financial tool there is. This calculator takes your take-home income and your major expense categories and shows you, in one glance, whether your month balances — and how much is genuinely left to save.

The formula

Remaining = Total income − Total expenses
Savings rate = (Remaining ÷ Total income) × 100

Worked example

If you take home $4,000 and spend $3,200, you have $800 left over — a 20% savings rate, which is the widely used benchmark for healthy finances.

Frequently asked questions

Use your take-home (net) pay — the amount that actually lands in your account. Using gross income makes your budget look better than it is.
Many financial planners suggest 20% of net income. If you are paying off high-interest debt, that payoff can reasonably substitute for part of it.
A common guideline is 30% of net income or less. Above that, other categories usually have to be squeezed.
