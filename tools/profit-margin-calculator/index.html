How this calculator works

Margin and markup are the most commonly confused pair of terms in business pricing. Margin compares profit to revenue; markup compares profit to cost. Confusing them leads to underpricing.

The formula

Profit = Revenue − Cost
Margin = (Profit ÷ Revenue) × 100
Markup = (Profit ÷ Cost) × 100

Worked example

Selling for $120 what cost $72 gives $48 profit — a 40% margin but a 66.7% markup.
