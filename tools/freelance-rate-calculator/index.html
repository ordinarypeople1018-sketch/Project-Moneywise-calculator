How this calculator works

The classic freelance mistake is dividing a salary by 2,080 hours. Freelancers pay their own tax, cover their own expenses, and only bill a fraction of their working time. This calculator accounts for all three.

The formula

Billable hours = Billable hrs/week × Working weeks
Revenue needed = (Income + Expenses) ÷ (1 − Tax rate)
Hourly rate = Revenue needed ÷ Billable hours

Worked example

Wanting $60,000 take-home with $8,000 expenses, 25% tax and 1,150 billable hours requires about $79 per hour.
