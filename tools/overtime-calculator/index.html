How this calculator works

Overtime pay rules differ enormously around the world — from no statutory requirement to double time on public holidays. This calculator lets you enter the multiplier that applies to you.

The formula

Overtime rate = Hourly wage × Multiplier
Overtime pay = Overtime rate × Overtime hours
Total = Regular pay + Overtime pay

Worked example

$22/hour with 8 hours at 1.5× gives $121 in overtime pay on top of $880 regular pay.
