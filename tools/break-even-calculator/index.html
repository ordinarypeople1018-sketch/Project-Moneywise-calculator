How this calculator works

Break-even is the number of units you must sell before you stop losing money. It is the single most important number for any new product or business.

The formula

Contribution per unit = Price − Variable cost
Break-even units = Fixed costs ÷ Contribution per unit

Worked example

With $8,000 fixed costs, $12 variable cost and a $30 price, you need 445 units to break even.

Frequently asked questions

The money each sale leaves after covering its own variable cost — the amount that goes toward fixed costs and profit.
