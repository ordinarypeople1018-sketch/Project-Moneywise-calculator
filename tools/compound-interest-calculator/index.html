How this calculator works

Compound interest means your interest earns interest. Over long periods this snowball effect can dwarf the money you actually put in — which is why starting early matters far more than starting big.

The formula

Balance = P(1+r)ⁿ + PMT × [((1+r)ⁿ − 1) ÷ r]

P = initial amount, PMT = monthly contribution,
r = monthly rate, n = number of months

Worked example

$5,000 plus $200/month at 7% for 20 years grows to roughly $112,000 — of which only $53,000 is money you contributed.
