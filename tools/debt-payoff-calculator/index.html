How this calculator works

Two strategies dominate debt payoff. Avalanche minimises interest by attacking the highest rate first. Snowball builds momentum by clearing the smallest balance first. Both work — the best one is the one you will actually stick to.

The formula

Each month: accrue interest, pay all minimums,
then throw the entire surplus at the target debt.
Target = highest rate (avalanche) or lowest balance (snowball)

Worked example

With $10,500 across three debts and $250 extra per month, avalanche typically clears the debt several months sooner and saves hundreds in interest versus minimum payments alone.
