How this calculator works

Subscriptions are designed to be invisible — small, automatic and easy to forget. Multiplying them out over several years shows their true weight.

The formula

Annual cost = Monthly cost × 12
Total cost = Annual cost × Years

Worked example

$85 per month is $1,020 per year, and $5,100 over five years.
