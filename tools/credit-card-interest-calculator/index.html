How this calculator works

Credit cards are the most expensive common form of borrowing. Because minimum payments are a percentage of a shrinking balance, they fall over time — which is why balances can linger for decades.

The formula

Monthly interest = Balance × (APR ÷ 12)
Minimum payment = max(Balance × Min%, Floor)
Principal reduction = Payment − Interest

Worked example

A $3,500 balance at 24% APR paying 2% minimum takes over 20 years to clear and costs more in interest than the original balance.
