How this calculator works

Stacked discounts are deliberately confusing. Two 20% discounts sound like 40% off but actually total 36%, because the second discount applies to the reduced price.

The formula

After first = Price × (1 − d₁ ÷ 100)
Final = After first × (1 − d₂ ÷ 100)
Effective discount = (1 − Final ÷ Price) × 100

Worked example

$249 with 20% off then another 10% off comes to $179.28 — a combined 28% discount, not 30%.

Frequently asked questions
