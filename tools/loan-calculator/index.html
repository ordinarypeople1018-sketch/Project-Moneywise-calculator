How this calculator works

A loan calculator shows the true cost of borrowing. The monthly payment tells you what you commit to; the total interest tells you what the loan actually costs.

The formula

M = P × r ÷ (1 − (1+r)⁻ⁿ)

M = monthly payment, P = principal,
r = monthly interest rate, n = number of months

Worked example

A $20,000 loan at 8% over 5 years costs $405.53 per month and $4,332 in interest.
