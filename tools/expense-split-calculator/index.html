How this calculator works

Splitting a bill is simple arithmetic, but tipping and rounding make it fiddly. This tool gives you an exact per-person figure.

The formula

Grand total = Bill × (1 + Tip% ÷ 100)
Per person = Grand total ÷ Number of people

Worked example

A $120 bill with a 15% tip is $138; split four ways that is $34.50 each.

Frequently asked questions

This version assumes equal splits. For unequal shares, calculate each person's subtotal separately and add their proportional tip.
