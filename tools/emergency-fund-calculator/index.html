How this calculator works

An emergency fund is money set aside specifically for the unexpected — a job loss, a medical bill, a car repair. It is the foundation that stops a single bad event from derailing your finances.

The formula

Target fund = Essential monthly expenses × Months of cover

Worked example

If your essentials cost $2,200 a month and you want 6 months of cover, your target is $13,200.
