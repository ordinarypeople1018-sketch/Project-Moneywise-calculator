How this calculator works

A mortgage payment is more than principal and interest. Property taxes, insurance and maintenance all belong in the real monthly number — this calculator puts them together.

The formula

Loan = Price − Down payment
M = P × r ÷ (1 − (1+r)⁻ⁿ)
Total monthly = P&I + Tax/12 + Insurance/12 + HOA

Worked example

A $320,000 home with 20% down at 6.5% over 30 years: the loan is $256,000 and principal + interest is about $1,618 per month.
