About MONEYWISE

MONEYWISE exists to make everyday financial decisions clearer. Most people are never taught how a loan actually amortises, why compound interest matters, or what a savings rate should look like. The result is a lot of avoidable stress and a lot of avoidable cost.

Every tool here is free, works without registration, and runs entirely in your browser. Nothing you type into a calculator is transmitted anywhere.

What this is

  • A set of 22 calculators built on standard, verifiable financial formulas.
  • Plain-language guides that explain the reasoning behind the numbers.
  • An international tool by default — 31 currencies with locale-correct formatting.

What this is not

  • Not financial, investment, tax or legal advice.
  • Not personalised — the calculators cannot know your full circumstances.
  • Not a substitute for a qualified professional when the stakes are high.

Editorial principles

No fabricated statistics. No invented testimonials. No fake urgency. Where a number depends on assumptions — investment returns, tax rates, overtime multipliers — the tool lets you set the assumption yourself rather than pretending there is one universal answer.

Technical honesty

Calculations handle zero, negative and extremely large inputs. Currency formatting is kept strictly separate from arithmetic, so changing your display currency never changes a result. The currency converter shows an error rather than a stale rate, because a confidently wrong number is worse than no number.
